Crafted for Serious Readers

An OS-Level Reading Superpower

Shelf merges the tactile speed of native Rust engineering with generative AI reading assistance.

Bilingual AI Dictionary

Highlight any word to instantly view clear definitions in English alongside authentic Hindi translations, contextual meanings, and audio pronunciation.

Interactive AI Inquire

Chat conversationally with Gemini or OpenRouter right inside the popup card. Ask follow-up questions, request historical nuances, or simplify convoluted sentences.

Unified Book Memory

Every saved word, quote, personal note, highlight, and underline is indexed in a persistent sidebar. Click any page badge to jump and flash the exact passage.

3 Reading Modes

Switch effortlessly between distraction-free Single Page, a realistic two-page Book Spread with 3D page-flip animations, or Continuous Free Vertical Scroll.

Highlights & Underlines

Precision word annotations that highlight only the exact targeted word or sentence without spilling across the document. Includes dedicated underline mode.

Privacy-First Architecture

Zero telemetry. All books, reading sessions, notes, and vocabulary are stored locally in an embedded SQLite database. Bring your own free API keys safely.
