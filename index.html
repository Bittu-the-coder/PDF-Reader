How it works

Understand more, remember longer

Shelf turns every hard word and dense paragraph into something you understand — and then helps you keep it.

Select a word

Double-click any word. Its meaning, a translation in your language and what it means in this sentence appear right next to it.

Go deeper

Select a whole passage to explain, translate or summarize it, or ask a follow-up question — all without leaving the page.

Keep it

Save words, highlights and notes to the book's memory. Saved words return as flashcards just before you'd forget them.

Features

Everything a serious reader needs

A calm, distraction-free reader with the tools you actually use — and none of the clutter.

Meanings in place

Definitions, translations in Hindi, Spanish, French or any language you choose, the meaning in context, examples and pronunciation.

Explain, translate, summarize

Select a passage to get a plain-language explanation, a line-by-line translation or the key points — then ask follow-up questions.

Find in book

Press Ctrl F to search every page. Matches are highlighted on the page and listed with their sentence and chapter.

Word review

Saved words come back as flashcards with spaced repetition — guess the word from your own book's sentence, then reveal the meaning.

Highlights & notes

Six highlight colours plus underlines that land exactly on your text. Notes, highlights and words are grouped by page in Book Memory.

Links & contents that work

Click links inside the PDF, browse the full nested table of contents, and hop straight back with Alt ←.

Bookmarks & reading time

Bookmark pages with Ctrl D. See how many minutes are left in the chapter, based on your own reading speed.

Read your way

Single page, two-page book spread or continuous scroll. Paper, sepia, warm, night and OLED page colours. Pinch to zoom.

Export your notes

Save every highlight, note and word of a book as a tidy Markdown study sheet, organised by chapter.

Private by design

No account, no tracking. Books, notes and highlights stay on your computer. Only the text you look up goes to the AI provider you choose.

A library you'll enjoy

Your books on a 3D wooden bookshelf with covers and progress, collections, and one click to continue where you stopped.

Small & fast

A native app of about 7 MB built with Rust. Opens big PDFs quickly and stays light on memory.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Shelf free?

Yes. Shelf is free and open source under the MIT license. The AI lookups use your own API key — Google Gemini and many OpenRouter models have a free tier that is plenty for reading.

Do I need an API key to read?

No. Reading, highlights, notes, bookmarks, search and export all work without one. A key is only needed for meanings, translations and explanations.

Where is my data stored?

Everything — your library, notes, highlights and saved words — lives in a local database on your computer. API keys are kept in your system's credential store (Windows Credential Manager, macOS Keychain or Secret Service on Linux).

Which languages can it translate into?

Any language the AI model knows. Hindi, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Chinese, Russian, Arabic, Portuguese and Italian are one click away, and you can type any other language in Settings.

Can I set Shelf as my default PDF app?

Yes. The installers register Shelf for .pdf files — choose it under “Open with” and tick “Always use this app”.
